CompletedData analysis · Machine learning

High-Value Customer Identification for a Fintech Bank

Which of 10,000 bank customers are worth the most today, and which ones could be worth more tomorrow? I built a weighted Customer Value Score and a K-Means segmentation to answer both.

  • Explored a 26-column Customer 360 dataset (no missing values or duplicates) and found strong links between income and spend (r = 0.75), balance (0.71) and investment (0.71).
  • Clustered customers with K-Means (k = 4) on 12 standardised features into Standard, Silver, Gold and Premium; the model agreed with existing Premium labels 94.5% of the time.
  • Flagged 292 top-10%-income customers who weren't Premium, and ranked 1,338 low-value, high-potential customers into High, Medium and Low priority with an action for each.
